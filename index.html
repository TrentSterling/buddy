HOW TO PLAY

Drag to grab, release to throw. Right-drag always grabs, even with a weapon equipped. Click INVITE for your room link. Purchases are shared with every player.

E tools · U hide controls · 1–0 toys · Q / W / F / X / T heavy tools · J tether · V bumper · C workshop · H repair Buddy · M wash room · B basketball · R stand · G gravity · Enter chat · D diagnostics.

The host can switch to SANDBOX in the workshop to open every tool. On touch screens, tap your current tool to open the rack. Your mouse stays visible by default; the optional game cursor only hides it inside the play area. Leaving the play area releases your tool. Blood, sound, and screen shake are local preferences.

CREDITS & TECH

Inspired by Interactive Buddy by Shock-Value. Buddy Bonk is an independent multiplayer sandbox, not an official sequel.

Networking uses Trystero 0.25.4 by Dan Motzenbecker, under the MIT license. Canvas 2D artwork, synthesized audio, and host-authoritative physics run in your browser.

Host workshop progress, player name, and local preferences are saved in this browser when storage is available. No account, analytics, or advertising is added by this game. Public discovery contacts third-party services; room messages travel between peers.

Public discovery and direct peer connections depend on your network. Host departure ends the room. Keep the host window visible.
